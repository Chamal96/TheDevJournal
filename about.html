Senior AI/ML Engineer · Data Engineer · AWS · MSc Data Science and AI (reading)

I’m Chamal Jayasinghe, a Senior Data Science Engineer at QrioMatrix. I have a BSc (Hons) in Applied Statistics and Mathematics from the University of Peradeniya, and I write here about the work of building with data, models, and cloud systems.

I’m passionate about designing, building, and maintaining scalable data pipelines on AWS. That includes RDS, Redshift, DynamoDB, EMR, DMS, Kinesis, Glue, Lambda, Athena, PySpark, Hadoop, Hive, and Python — for both real-time and batch processing — so data stays accessible, reliable, and secure.

I also work in machine learning and deep learning, especially TensorFlow for training and deploying models. That covers NLP for text, plus object detection and image classification, and folding those models into data pipelines for analytics and predictive insight.

This journal is the public notebook for that craft: what I’m building, what I’m learning, and the details that usually stay behind a laptop.
